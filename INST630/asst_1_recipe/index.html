Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Crispy edges, chewy centers, loaded with chocolate

These classic chocolate chip cookies are crispy on the edges, chewy in the middle, and packed with chocolate chips. Perfect for any occasion — from lunch boxes to holiday gatherings. This foolproof recipe has been tested countless times and always delivers delicious results.

Prep 15 min
Cook 12 min
Total 27 min
Yield 24 cookies
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Preheat oven

    Heat to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.

  2. 2
    Mix dry ingredients

    In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.

  3. 3
    Cream butter and sugars

    In a large bowl, beat softened butter with both sugars until light and fluffy, about 2–3 minutes.

  4. 4
    Add eggs and vanilla

    Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla extract.

  5. 5
    Combine wet and dry

    Gradually blend in the flour mixture until just combined. Do not overmix.

  6. 6
    Fold in chocolate chips

    Stir in chocolate chips and walnuts if using.

  7. 7
    Shape and bake

    Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto prepared baking sheets, spacing 2 inches apart. Bake 9–11 minutes until golden brown.

  8. 8
    Cool

    Let cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
